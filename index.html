GOVERNANCE & RISK

Autonomy with boundaries.

Financial autonomy should not mean unrestricted authority. Alia is designed around bounded permissions, independent review and owner-defined controls.

Human-defined authority

The owner determines what Alia is allowed to access, spend, trade and automate.

Capital limits

Trading systems operate inside explicit allocation and drawdown constraints.

Execution safeguards

Preview protections, fee limits, reconciliation and kill-switch controls remain part of the execution path.

Auditability

Research, decisions, worker outcomes and financial actions are designed to leave traceable evidence.
